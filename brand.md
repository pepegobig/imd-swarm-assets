# Lobby x IMD Swarm brand

The exact palette of `infographics/swarm-content-creator.png` (from the script that rendered it).

## Core palette

| Role | Hex | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Background (deep navy) | `#0A0F2E` | Full canvas |
| Card fill | `#111A42` at 82% opacity | The four step cards |
| Card border | `#25305F` | 1 px outline around each card |
| Primary text | `#F3F5FF` | Headline line 1, step titles, footer line |
| Muted text | `#A3ABD6` | Step subtitles, "in minutes", the "×" |
| Orange | `#F2A23A` | Gradient start, bounties, the "Buys IMD" ring |
| Hot pink | `#EC1F63` | Gradient end, "Burned" |
| Cyan | `#38D6E0` | IMD Swarm pill, prompt box, views, contract |
| Green | `#4FDC7C` | Upvotes |
| Gold highlight | `#FFD27A` | "Earnings" label, coin tops |
| Coin shadow (dark amber) | `#B86E14` | Sides of the coin stack |
| Arrow grey-blue | `#5D6AA8` | The → connectors |

## Signature gradient

Orange to pink at 110°: `#F2A23A` → `#EC1F63`. It is used on "a content creator." in the headline, the numbered step
badges, the send button (with a pink glow), the "AUTO-POSTED" pill and the URL pill in the footer.

The flame uses a vertical version: `#EC1F63` at the bottom, `#F2713A` in the middle, `#F2A23A` at the top, and a
`#FFD27A` → `#FFF3C4` inner core.

## Background glows

Three soft radial glows on the navy, each fading to transparent: hot pink `#EC1F63` at 30% opacity top-right, cyan
`#38D6E0` at 20% left middle, orange `#F2A23A` at 20% bottom-right.

## Accent tiles

Icon tiles use the accent color at 12% opacity as the fill, with a 1.5 px border in the full accent color (for example
the Views tile: cyan at 12% with a solid cyan border).

## Type

- Headline, LOBBY wordmark, badge numbers: Archivo Black
- Body and titles: Outfit (400 to 800)
- Labels (PROMPT, VIEWS, CONTRACT…) and the URL: JetBrains Mono, all caps, wide letter spacing

All three are free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

## Prompt-ready version

Dark neon-tech style. Deep navy background (#0A0F2E) with soft radial glows: hot pink (#EC1F63) top-right, cyan
(#38D6E0) left, orange (#F2A23A) bottom-right. Navy cards (#111A42) with thin #25305F borders and rounded corners.
Signature orange-to-pink gradient (#F2A23A → #EC1F63) for highlights, badges and pills. Accent colors: cyan #38D6E0,
green #4FDC7C, gold #FFD27A. Text is off-white (#F3F5FF), and secondary text is lavender-grey (#A3ABD6). Bold heavy
sans-serif headlines, clean geometric body text and monospace all-caps labels.

Short form for tools that prefer names: midnight navy, hot pink, sunset orange, electric cyan, neon green, gold.
